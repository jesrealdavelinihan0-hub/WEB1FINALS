BIOGRAPHY

Yumi Sukihero

Yami Sukehiro 「ヤミ・スケヒロ」, born as Sukehiro Yami 「夜見介大 Yami Sukehiro」, is a descendant of the Yami Clan from the Hino Country. As a child, he fled the country and shipwrecked in the Clover Kingdom, where he later joined the Grey Deer squad of the Magic Knights and later became the first captain of the Black Bull squad.

His Magic

ami uses this rare magic attribute to generate and manipulate the element of darkness. He primarily uses this magic by channeling it into his katana. This magic has the ability to draw in other forms of magic, namely Light Magic, absorbing it into the darkness and can even affect the other world. Due to his Dark Magic's ability to affect the other world naturally, Yami is ranked as an Arcane Stage mage.

Apearance

Yami is a tall man with a very muscular build. He has grey eyes and black hair of medium length that is messily combed backward, making it stick outwards. Additionally, he sports a stubble mustache and beard.

About Us

As the captain of the Black Bull squad, Yami wears a black banner with the squad's insignia on it. He wears it over his right shoulder and it is attached by two strings to his left shoulder. The banner also has tattered edges, seeming to have been ripped off from a bigger banner. After six months of training, Yami's black banner is now longer to where he now wears it over his right shoulder and under his left arm in a cape-like fashion.

In Yami's youth in the Hino Country, he wore a short kimono with black leggings
